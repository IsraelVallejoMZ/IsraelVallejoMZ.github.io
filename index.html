Blue Carbon · Remote Sensing · Featured

Mangrove Loss & Blue Carbon Stock Analysis (1996–2020)

Spatiotemporal analysis of mangrove forest loss and blue carbon implications across the Mekong Delta (Vietnam) and Mexican Pacific Coast (Oaxaca–Chiapas), using GMW v3.0 satellite data, IPCC Tier 1 carbon coefficients, and Mann-Kendall trend analysis. Combined carbon loss: 6.68 Mt C over 24 years.

R QGIS GMW v3.0 Mann-Kendall IPCC

Remote Sensing · Multi-Sensor · Coastal Change

Multi-Sensor Coastal Cover Change — Ao Phang Nga Bay, Thailand

Integration of Sentinel-1 SAR and Sentinel-2 optical imagery to detect vegetation dynamics across a 7-year period in a tropical mangrove system. Cross-sensor Wilcoxon validation confirmed statistically significant change across NDVI, EVI, and SAR VV (p < 0.05).
